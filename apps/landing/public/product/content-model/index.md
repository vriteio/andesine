# Content model

Structure that writers love. And apps rely on. Add fields to your content and keep every entry consistent.

## Organized from the start, and consistent by design

- Folders that make sense: nest collections as deep as you need.
- Fields for anything: text, numbers, dates, tags, and more.
- More than one body: split an entry into parts, like a summary and the main text.
- One schema, every entry: define fields once and apply them to a whole collection.
- Find by any field: filter search by status, date, or any other field.
- Ready for your code: generate types from your schemas and use them in your app.
