# API & CLI

Your content, in your code. Any way you like. An API, a TypeScript SDK, and a CLI, with types from your schemas.

## Every way in

- HTTP API: JSON in, JSON out, with keys you control.
- TypeScript SDK: a method for every operation, in Node, browsers, and workers.
- CLI: sign in, call the API, and generate types from your terminal.

## Fits into your stack

- Webhooks: get a request when content changes or goes live.
- Typed content: generate types from your schemas, and keep them fresh in CI.
- Any format: convert content to Markdown, MDX, HTML, and more.
- Keys for every place: secret keys for servers, publishable keys for browsers.
