# Team knowledge base

Knowledge your team can find. And keep safe. Write together, find answers fast, and decide who sees what.

## Find anything

- Search as you type: find any page in a few keystrokes.
- Answers with sources: ask a question, and get an answer that links to its pages.
- Find by any field: filter by status, team, or any other field.

## You decide who sees what

- Roles and groups: give teams the access they need, and no more.
- Restricted collections: keep sensitive pages to the people who need them.
- Every version saved: see who changed what, and restore any version.
