# Headless CMS

Content for your site and app. Shaped to your schema. Blogs, changelogs, and in-app content, written in an editor and delivered through the API.

## Structured from the start

- Fields for anything: text, numbers, dates, tags, and more.
- One schema, every entry: define fields once for a whole collection.
- More than one body: a summary, the main text, and any other part you need.

## Delivered anywhere

- Content API: fetch entries and their fields into any site or app.
- Custom channels: serve previews, releases, and apps their own versions.
- Ready for your code: generate types from your schemas.
- Webhooks: rebuild your site when content goes live.
