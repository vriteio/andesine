# Andesine Pages

Your docs, ready to share. Published in a click. Host it with us, or yourself. Search and AI answers built in.

## Two ways to go live

- Live in a click: turn your docs into a site in one click. Every update goes live when you publish.
- Make it your own: start from the open-source template and shape every page to fit your product.

## Everything readers need

- Answers, not just results: readers ask a question and get an answer with sources.
- Looks like you: your logo, colors, and links on every page.
- On your domain: serve your docs from your own address.
